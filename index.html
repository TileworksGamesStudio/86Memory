Difficulty Tiers & Progression

• Apprentice: Relaxed count-up timer, standard 250pt matches.
• Bartender: Unlocks after 1 successful shift. 300pt matches with strict accuracy pacing.
• Head Bartender: Unlocks after mastering 4 drinks. High-intensity 75s Rush Hour with miss penalties.
